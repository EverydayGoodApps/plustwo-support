PlusTwo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

PlusTwo is provided by Ronnie Kanj under the name EverydayGoodApps. This policy explains how information is handled when you use PlusTwo version 1.0, visit this support website or contact us.

Your workout data

PlusTwo stores your workout plans, exercises, sets, weights, reps and workout history locally on your device. This information is used to display your workouts and calculate your next targets. It is not sent to servers operated by EverydayGoodApps.

Version 1.0 does not require a PlusTwo account and does not provide in-app cloud syncing. Your device's backup settings may allow Apple or a computer backup to store a copy of app data. Those backups are managed separately from PlusTwo.

Advertising and tracking

PlusTwo version 1.0 does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell your personal information or use your workout data for advertising.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include, such as your name, message, screenshots or device details. We use this information to respond, investigate problems and improve the app.

Support emails are handled through Gmail, provided by Google. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed to explain your request.

We keep support correspondence only for as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion.

This support website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. We do not add analytics scripts or advertising cookies to this website.

For details, see the GitHub Privacy Statement and Google Privacy Policy .

Deleting your information

You can delete workouts within PlusTwo or use the reset option in the app's settings to remove all saved workout data. Deleting data from the app does not necessarily remove copies held in existing device backups. Manage those through your backup provider's settings.

We cannot access or recover workout data stored only on your device.

Your privacy requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict its use. Contact us using the address below to make a request. You may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If PlusTwo's features or data practices change, we will update this policy and its revision date.

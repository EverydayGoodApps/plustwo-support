Common questions

How do I get started?

Tap Create Your First Workout, choose your weekly schedule, then add exercises and set your target weights and reps. Enter your achieved weight and reps as you train.

How do I earn +2 kg?

Meet both the target weight and target reps in every working set of an exercise. When the next week is created, that exercise’s working-set target weights increase by 2 kg. You can adjust targets to suit your training.

What if I miss a target?

If any working set misses either target or has missing results, the exercise’s target weights stay the same next week.

Do warm-up sets count?

No. Turn on Warm-up for those sets. They are excluded from the +2 kg calculation, and their target weights stay unchanged.

How do I move to the next week?

Complete or skip every workout in the current week. PlusTwo then creates the next week. Skipped workouts keep their target weights unchanged.

Where are my workouts saved?

Workouts are saved locally on your device. Version 1.0 does not provide in-app cloud syncing. Any device backups are managed separately through your Apple settings.
